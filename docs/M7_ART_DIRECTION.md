# M7 — Art Direction Pass (character readability)

**Status:** **WORKING PRODUCTION BASELINE (refinable).** This direction is adopted as the baseline
for continued content production. It is not a permanent lock on every visual detail — the
product/art owner can still refine it — but new content should be authored consistently with it so
the match set reads coherently. The pipeline and anatomy contract are unchanged; this pass refines
the prototype-looking forms.

---

## 1. Constraints this direction satisfies (from `GAME_CONCEPT.md` §20)

- stylized, readable and expressive — not ultra-realistic;
- more grounded/detailed than Brawl Stars, not a military simulation;
- Valorant-level readability at gameplay distance;
- strong, immediately legible competitive silhouettes;
- P1 head clearly exposed (only critical/headshot region);
- a visually distinct P2 shoulder/arms layer that never replaces P1's head.

## 2. Proposed direction — “grounded stylized tactical sci-fi”

- **Forms:** layered matte armour with restrained panel detail; beveled, slightly chunky shapes
  rather than spheres or tubes; deliberately readable at 20–40 m.
- **Joints:** **articulated mechanical sleeves**, not bare ball joints (shoulder/elbow sleeves,
  vambraces, knee pads). This reads as a machine/joint system rather than a toy.
- **Palette:** neutral armour greys as the base, with **one high-contrast team accent** per pair
  (cool teal = Alpha, warm orange = Beta) used sparingly on trims, visors and grip points.
- **Emissive language:** visors and sensor lenses are the only strong emissive elements, reinforcing
  the exposed-head and sensor read.
- **Role read:** P1 is the dominant humanoid body silhouette; P2 is a mechanical upper-chest /
  clavicle / shoulder rig with two clearly articulated arms. Mismatched P1/P2 skins are intentional
  and must still read as one fighter made of two people.

## 3. What changed in this pass

- P2 shoulder pods and elbows replaced with angular **pauldrons** and **joint sleeves**; all arms
  gained forearm **vambraces** and knuckle plates.
- P1 torso gained layered side plates, chest/back plates, a belt buckle and a chest accent strip;
  legs gained **knee pads** and shin strips.
- P1 head reworked for a clearer exposed read: visible neck and neck guard, jaw, brow, eye visor,
  crest and ear plates — nothing covers the face or silhouette.
- P2 still has **no head, helmet or backpack**; its cosmetic sensor remains small, offset and below
  P1's head.

All gameplay constraints are preserved: the M5 rig contract, anchors, authoritative hitboxes,
gameplay-stat invariance, the 1:1 Blender → Unity pipeline and the weapon-grip check are unchanged
and still pass.

## 4. Status of this decision

The direction above is the **working production baseline** for continuing content work (character
skins, map theming, material language). It is deliberately not a final lock: refinements to palette,
theme and material detail are expected and allowed. What should remain stable is the *readability
contract* (§1) and the M5/M6 rig contract, both of which are enforced by validators. No blanket
visual identity is treated as permanently frozen by M7.
