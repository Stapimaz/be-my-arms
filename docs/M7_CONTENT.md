# M7 — Audio, VFX, Maps and Content Scale

**Status:** **Systems complete; the roadmap “production quality” acceptance bar is only partially
met.** The production maps, match integration, character rigs and the content pipeline are
production-ready for continued work. The **audio, music and VFX remain production-test placeholders**
and are explicitly **not** claimed as production quality.
**Modules:** `Assets/Scripts/M7` (`BeMyArms.M7` + `BeMyArms.M7.Editor`), `art/audio`,

## 2. Maps — production Duel/2v2 arenas (production-ready)

A modular **map kit** of 15 grid-aligned pieces is authored in Blender (`make_mapkit.py`) and
imported into Unity: floor, full/half wall, doorway, low/high cover, platform, ramp, railing,
catwalk, crate, team spawn pads, pillar and corner. Two arenas are composed from it and are
**match-ready scenes** (shared NetworkManager/director bootstrap + matchmaker host + map spawn data):

| Map | Family | Bounds | Spawns | Cover | Lanes | Verticality | Spawn clearance |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `M7DuelArena.unity` | Duel | 24 × 24 m | 4 | 13 | 4 | 3.04 m | 2.31 m |
| `M7TwoVsTwoArena.unity` | 2v2 | 32 × 32 m | 8 | 19 | 6 | 3.04 m | 2.82 m |

The **map family** strategy (concept §17.2) is explicit: the Duel variant closes the east/west
flanks; the 2v2 variant opens them as extra lanes. Each arena writes a validated `M7MapDefinition`
record (family, bounds, team/body/role spawns, cover/lane/verticality, spawn clearance) and links it
into the scene (`M7MapSceneLink`) and into the gameplay layer (`M3MapSpawns`). `M7MapValidator`
enforces complete spawns inside the play area, cover ≥ 6, lanes ≥ 2, verticality ≥ 1 m and
**spawn clearance ≥ 0.8 m** (so cover/walls never overlap a spawn).

### Networked verification (the real acceptance evidence)

Both arenas were run through the **actual dedicated-server + client match loop**, not just the
structural validators:

- `M7DuelArena`: dedicated server + 4 clients (2 bodies) → `using map spawns (4)`, buy → live →
  eliminations → `MATCH END winner=A score A=3 B=0`.
- `M7TwoVsTwoArena`: dedicated server + 8 clients with matchmaking and role ratings →
  `matchmaking (TwoVsTwo) quality=0.0 teamA=1050 teamB=1050`, 8 humans, `using map spawns (8)`,
  first-to-3 match, per-role rating deltas.

No runtime errors; bodies spawned at the arena spawn points and navigated the cover/verticality
layout. This surfaced and fixed a real defect: a high-cover block previously overlapped the Duel
spawn positions (clearance ≈ 0); it was relocated and the clearance check added.

## 3. Audio (production-test placeholder — not production quality)

A reproducible procedural audio set is generated by `art/audio/make_sfx.py` (run through Blender's
bundled Python via `tools/pipeline/build-audio.ps1`): 18 SFX and 3 loops. `M7AudioImportSettings`
applies production import settings; `M7AudioLibrary` maps every `M7AudioId` to a clip and
`M7AudioService` plays pooled 2D/3D SFX and looping beds.

**Honest assessment:** these are synthesised noise/tone bursts. They are functional and complete
enough to exercise every gameplay event, but they are **not** production-quality audio. Final audio
sourcing, recording, mixing, music composition and any voice provider (concept §19) are **open and
deferred**.

## 4. VFX (production-test placeholder — not production quality)

`M7VfxPrefabBuilder` builds 11 pooled particle effects; `M7VfxLibrary` maps every `M7VfxId` to an
effect and `M7VfxService` spawns/attaches/auto-despawns them.

**Honest assessment:** these are code-built particle systems — readable and correct, but placeholder
quality. They are **not** production VFX. Authored effects, timing/polish and VFX Graph work are
deferred; the runtime API is stable so they can be replaced without code changes.

## 5. Content scale — structural manifest (not quality evidence)

`M7ContentManifest` + `M7ContentValidator` confirm the project contains the pieces for a match set
(`MATCH SET READY`). **This is a structural completeness check, not evidence of subjective production
quality** — a count of clips/effects cannot establish that the content is good.
