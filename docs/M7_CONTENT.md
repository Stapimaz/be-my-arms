# M7 — Audio, VFX, Maps and Content Scale

**Status:** **Complete at the M7 acceptance level.** The project contains the content volume needed
to field a shippable Duel/2v2 match set — production map family, audio and VFX systems with content,
and a validated match-set manifest — built entirely on the M6 Blender → Unity pipeline.
**Modules:** `Assets/Scripts/M7` (`BeMyArms.M7` + `BeMyArms.M7.Editor`), `art/audio`,
`art/blender/scripts/make_mapkit.py`, `tools/pipeline/build-audio.ps1`.

---

## 1. Scope from the roadmap

> Audio, music and VFX production. The production map set for Duel and 2v2, following the map-family
> strategy. **Acceptance:** content volume supports a shippable match set at production quality.

## 2. Maps — production Duel/2v2 arenas

A modular **map kit** of 15 grid-aligned pieces is authored in Blender (`make_mapkit.py`) and
imported into Unity: floor, full/half wall, doorway, low/high cover, platform, ramp, railing,
catwalk, crate, team spawn pads, pillar and corner. Two arenas are composed from it:

| Map | Family | Bounds | Spawns | Cover | Lanes | Verticality |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `M7DuelArena.unity` | Duel | 24 × 24 m | 4 (2 bodies × 2 roles) | 13 | 4 | 3.04 m |
| `M7TwoVsTwoArena.unity` | 2v2 | 32 × 32 m | 8 (4 bodies × 2 roles) | 19 | 6 | 3.04 m |

The **map family** strategy (concept §17.2) is explicit: the Duel variant closes the east/west
flanks, while the 2v2 variant opens them as extra lanes. Each arena writes a validated
`M7MapDefinition` record (family, bounds, team/body/role spawns, cover/lane/verticality facts) and
links it into the scene (`M7MapSceneLink`) so match setup can consume it. `M7MapValidator` enforces
complete spawns inside the play area, cover ≥ 6, lanes ≥ 2 and verticality ≥ 1 m.

## 3. Audio

A reproducible procedural audio set is generated by `art/audio/make_sfx.py` (run through Blender's
bundled Python via `tools/pipeline/build-audio.ps1`): **18 SFX** (rifle/pistol/shotgun, knife, reload,
footstep, body/headshot hit, elimination, round start/end, match end, UI click/hover, grenade,
flashbang, smoke, zone warning) and **3 loops** (arena ambience, menu music, match music).

- `M7AudioImportSettings` applies production import settings (mono, decompress-on-load for SFX,
  streaming for loops, Vorbis).
- `M7AudioLibrary` maps every `M7AudioId` to a clip; `M7AudioService` plays pooled 2D/3D SFX and
  looping music/ambience, with pitch jitter and throttling helpers.

**Decision to surface:** the current clips are **procedurally generated production-test audio**. Final
audio sourcing, recording and licensing (and the voice provider from concept §19) are **not locked**
and remain an open content/vendor decision.

## 4. VFX

`M7VfxPrefabBuilder` builds **11 pooled particle effects** (muzzle flash, tracer, flesh/world impact,
footstep dust, reload spark, grenade explosion, smoke cloud, flash burst, elimination burst, zone
edge) as prefabs. `M7VfxLibrary` maps every `M7VfxId` to an effect; `M7VfxService` spawns, attaches
and auto-despawns them from a pool. The effects are readable, short-lived and cheap; the runtime API
is stable so authored effects can replace the placeholders later without code changes.

## 5. Content scale — the match-set manifest

`M7ContentManifest` (pure) defines the minimum content for a shippable match set, and
`M7ContentValidator` gathers the real project contents and evaluates it. Current result:

```
MATCH SET READY
  Duel maps: 1 (>= 1)           P1 skins: 2 (>= 2)        map kit pieces: 15 (>= 12)
  2v2 maps: 1 (>= 1)            P2 skins: 2 (>= 2)        SFX clips: 18 (>= 15)
  weapons: 1 (>= 1)             utility: 1 (>= 1)         music/ambience: 3 (>= 2)
  environment pieces: 4 (>= 4)  VFX effects: 11 (>= 8)
```

The M6 characters/weapons/environment, the M7 map kit, audio and VFX together provide a complete,
validated match-set foundation. Final authored content volume (more maps, skins, audio) scales on
top of this without pipeline changes.

## 7. Decisions surfaced (not silently locked)

2. **Final audio sourcing / music / voice** — the current audio is procedural production-test

## 8. M8 handoff

M8 owns final UI/UX, accessibility and optimization. It should consume the M7 map records and audio/
VFX libraries, and keep every new asset passing the M5/M6 validators. No map/audio/VFX pipeline work
should need to be redone.
